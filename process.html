What you get

  • 2-week assessment of your current operations
  • Documentation of manual workflows and bottlenecks
  • Prioritized list of automation opportunities
  • ROI estimates for each improvement

Timeline and scope

2 weeks from kickoff to final report
Fixed price scoped based on team size and complexity

  • Access to our team for ad-hoc requests

Timeline and scope

Ongoing month-to-month or annual commitment
Flexible scope adjusts as your operations grow
Deliverable continuous improvement and new capabilities
