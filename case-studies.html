Da World Boutique: Product catalog automation

Problem

500-product catalog with inconsistent descriptions, weak SEO, and misaligned brand voice. Manual updates were time-consuming and error-prone.

Build

Deployed AI-assisted product description workflow using custom prompts with the open-source LLaVA model. Built standardization process, then refined output to match boutique's brand identity.

Result

  • Refreshed all 500 product descriptions in 4 weeks
  • Established repeatable workflow for ongoing updates
  • Improved SEO-friendly copy across entire storefront
  • Standardized brand voice and product information

Timeline

4-week engagement: 1 week discovery and setup, 2 weeks bulk processing, 1 week refinement and training.
