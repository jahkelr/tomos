Automation Consulting Tiers

Choose the engagement level that matches your automation journey—from a quick-turn audit to ongoing orchestration.

Talk through the tiers Start with Audit, scale into Core Automations, and stay supported with Automation Management.

The three-tier model

Audit

10-business-day assessment that uncovers the highest-impact automation opportunities in your workflows.

  • Workflow discovery sessions with key stakeholders
  • Process maps contrasting current and automated future states
  • Prioritized backlog of 3–5 initiatives with complexity and impact scoring
  • ROI forecast detailing savings assumptions and quick wins

Investment: $500 flat

Core Automations

4–6 week build cycles that ship the highest-value automations identified during the audit.

  • Solution design aligned to your stack, data, and compliance requirements
  • Implementation of up to three priority workflows with QA and documentation
  • Change enablement support so teams adopt the new ways of working
  • Knowledge transfer for your operators or internal developers

Investment: $1,500 per sprint

Automation Management

Ongoing program management that keeps your automations healthy, compliant, and expanding.

  • Dedicated automation lead with monthly performance reviews
  • Proactive break/fix, model tuning, and integration monitoring
  • Quarterly roadmap planning with new use case launches
  • Governance support across security, data quality, and policy updates

Investment: Retainers starting at $2,500/mo

Where each tier fits

  • Audit: Perfect when you need a quantified business case, stakeholder alignment, and a clear starting point.
  • Core Automations: Ideal for teams ready to implement production-ready workflows without overloading internal engineers.
  • Automation Management: Best for organizations who want ongoing ownership, proactive optimization, and roadmap execution.

How engagements progress

  1. Discovery & prioritization

    We capture your goals, existing tooling, and success metrics during a 60-minute consult to confirm fit.

  2. Audit delivery

    You receive the process maps, backlog, and ROI model that guide near-term automation bets.

  3. Core implementation sprints

    We design, build, and launch the highest-leverage automations with testing, documentation, and enablement.

  4. Automation Management

    We operate and optimize the automations, monitor performance, and keep the roadmap moving.
