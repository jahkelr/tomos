Tier 01

AI Automation Audit

A 10-business-day deep dive that maps your workflows, spotlights the most costly bottlenecks, and quantifies where AI automation delivers the quickest ROI.

Deliverables

  • 60-minute discovery call for intake and alignment
  • Workflow mapping with bottleneck and failure-point analysis
  • Identification of 3–5 high-impact automations
  • Written AI Automation Plan with estimated ROI and roadmap

Value proposition

Gives you clarity on what to automate, the projected savings, and why it matters—before you invest in implementation.

Investment: $500 one-time

Tier 02

Core Automation Setup

A targeted build cycle where we design and implement your highest-value automations so they are production-ready without an ongoing contract.

Deliverables

  • Implementation of up to three core automations identified in the audit
  • Integrations with your existing tools (Sheets, CRMs, calendars, email, and more)
  • One onboarding and training session for your operators
  • Documentation so you can run and adjust automations independently

Value proposition

Delivers live automations that immediately save time and money—set up once, ready to use, no retainer required.

Investment: $1,500 one-time

Tier 03

Automation Management

Full-service stewardship where TOMOS monitors, maintains, and improves your automations so they scale with the business.

Deliverables

  • Everything included in Tier 2
  • Continuous monitoring, error handling, and updates
  • Monthly performance and ROI reporting
  • Unlimited in-scope automation adjustments plus priority support

Value proposition

Completely hands-off—TOMOS owns performance, compliance, and iteration so you only focus on the outcomes.

Investment: $2,500 setup + $1,000/month
